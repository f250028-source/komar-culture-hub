01 / WHO WE ARE

More Than a Hub.
A Community.

Komar Culture Hub (KCH) is a cultural initiative around Komar University that brings people together through creativity, culture, and intellectual engagement.

Through film screenings, book discussions, dramatic expression, and cultural activities, KCH encourages students to explore new perspectives, exchange ideas, and develop meaningful connections.
